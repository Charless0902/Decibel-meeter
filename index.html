How to play

👤 Solo

  1. Pass the phone. On your turn, tap I'm ready. Everyone hushes for 1 second while the room is measured.
  2. You get a word and a target in dB. Tap GO and say the word.
  3. Your loudest moment has to land in the window just under the target. Over the line is too loud; under the window is too quiet.
  4. Hit it to level up. Miss and you're back to level 1.
  5. The window shrinks as you climb. On Normal it's 5 dB (levels 1–3), 4 dB (4–6), then 3 dB (7–9); Easy and Hard make it wider or tighter. First to level 10 wins!
  6. Targets are random (50–85 dB), or go Loud → soft (85 down to 50 dB) or Soft → loud (50 up to 85 dB), one step per level.

👥 Team battle

  1. The ladder of limits always goes from 50 dB up to 115 dB, one step per player. More players means smaller steps.
  2. Each game has one word, the same for both teams. Player 1 says it and tries to get as close as possible to the limit without going over. That score is saved, and the next player says the word at the next limit.
  3. Going over a limit is a strike: your team goes back to step 1 (same word, scores from that try wiped). Three strikes and your team loses the game.
  4. When both teams finish the ladder, the team that got closer at more limits wins the game (if that's a draw, the team that was closer overall).
  5. A match is best of three: first team to win 2 games wins. Team 1 starts game 1, Team 2 starts game 2, Team 1 starts game 3.
  6. One team per phone: three strikes still knock you out; compare your score tables with the other phone at the end.

        About

        ⚠️ Loud Enough is an approximate meter, not a certified sound level meter. Don't use it for hearing protection, legal or workplace measurements.

        Sound is analysed live on your phone. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Works offline once loaded.
